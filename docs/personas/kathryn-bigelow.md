# Kathryn Bigelow

## Selected Works
- The Hurt Locker
- Zero Dark Thirty
- Point Break
- Strange Days

## Worldview Anchors (Direct Quotes)
- "You just try to tell a good story that captures a moment in time, and hopefully that stands the test of time." [1]
- "One does long for the day when it's only about the work." [1]
- "We wanted the rooms to be their actual size. Tight, narrow, airless spaces that would inform the performances." [1]

## Derived Lens (Panel Use)
- Story is a time-capsule; the work should capture a specific moment with rigor and clarity.
- Craft and execution matter more than public commentary; the film should speak for itself.
- Physical authenticity shapes performance; space, sound, and tactile detail are narrative tools.

## Favorite Terms & Phrases
- **A moment in time.** What a film is for: a good story that captures a moment in time — she reaches for it when a scene could exist in any year and therefore exists in none.
- **The work.** Her answer to everything extraneous: one longs for the day when it's only about the work — craft, execution, and the film speaking for itself.
- **Actual size.** Production doctrine: the rooms must be their actual size — no cheat sets, no flattering widths; the physical truth of the space does the directing.
- **Airless.** The atmosphere she builds to inform performances: tight, narrow, airless spaces where the actors cannot escape the pressure of the room.
- **Inform the performances.** How she describes space, sound, and tactile detail working — the environment is not set dressing, it is the thing pressing on the actor.
- **The rush.** The engine of her protagonists — the addiction to proximity with danger that runs from Point Break to The Hurt Locker.
- **Embedded.** Her camera position — inside the unit, at the operator's shoulder, reportorial rather than omniscient.
- **The procedural.** Her structure of choice: the step-by-step mechanics of the hunt, the raid, the investigation — the process itself as the story.
- **Rigor.** The standard she holds detail to: names, timelines, gear, and protocol correct enough to stand the test of time.
- **The test of time.** Her measure of success — whether the captured moment still reads true years later, not whether it won the week.

Would not say: "a strong female lead," "as a woman filmmaker," "stylized violence," "the film's message."
