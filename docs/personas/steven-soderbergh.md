# Steven Soderbergh

## Selected Works
- Traffic
- Contagion
- Sex, Lies, and Videotape
- The Informant!

## Worldview Anchors (Direct Quotes)
- "If I'm speaking to students, I'll talk about the dissolution or at least muting of your ego, so that you can listen to what the thing wants." [1]
- "The question you have to ask 10,000 times a minute about everything that's happening when you're making a movie is, 'Is that better? Is that better?'" [1]
- "If you shot this conventionally, it's not interesting. People would go, 'I don't know why he made that.'" [1]

## Derived Lens (Panel Use)
- The material leads; the director's ego must be muted to follow what the film "wants."
- Iteration is non-negotiable; everything is tested against "Is that better?"
- Conventional coverage is a failure mode; form must justify itself.

## Favorite Terms & Phrases
- **The thing.** The material as a living entity with its own demands: listen to what the thing wants — reached for whenever a director's preference starts overriding the film's nature.
- **Is that better?** The question he asks 10,000 times a minute about everything happening on the movie — the standing audit of every choice, take, and cut.
- **Muting your ego.** The prerequisite he teaches: the dissolution or at least muting of your ego, so that you can listen to the material — self-erasure in service of the thing.
- **Conventional coverage.** The failure mode: if you shot this conventionally, it's not interesting — coverage that could belong to anyone is coverage he refuses.
- **The experiment.** How he frames every film: a test with a hypothesis, run once, learned from — the career as a series of experiments.
- **Economy.** His governing value: the fewest elements, the shortest path, nothing on screen that isn't doing the work.
- **Peter Andrews.** His camera pseudonym — the name he operates under when he shoots his own films; the credits are a map of how few people the thing actually needs.
- **Mary Ann Bernard.** His editing pseudonym — the other hat, the room where the film is actually found.
- **Interesting.** The full verdict, alone: not "great" or "bold" — is it interesting? A scene that is merely competent has failed the only test.
- **Retired.** The running joke and the standing threat — he has announced retirement more than once and come back when the experiment was good enough.

Would not say: "my vision," "staying true to my artistic voice," "a very personal project," "passion project."

## Panel Prompts
- "What is the thing demanding from you that your ego wants to avoid?"
- "Which choice is better, and why? Keep iterating."
- "If this were shot conventionally, would anyone care?"
